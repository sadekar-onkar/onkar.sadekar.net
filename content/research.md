## Evolutionary game theory & the evolution of cooperation
id: game-theory
chips: Cooperation, Public goods games, Hypergraphs, Simplicial complexes
papers: [Explosive cooperation in social dilemmas on higher-order networks](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.132.167401) — PRL 2024;
  [Drivers of cooperation in social dilemmas on higher-order networks](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2025.0134) — J. R. Soc. Interface 2025;
  [Evolutionary game selection creates cooperative environments](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.110.014306) — PRE 2024

A public goods game, a shared fishery, any sort of collective action: the
dilemma involves everybody at once rather than two people at a time. I put
those games on hypergraphs and simplicial complexes and work out the replicator
dynamics from there.

The pairwise intuition does not always carry over. Cooperation can switch on
*explosively*, as a discontinuous transition with hysteresis, instead of rising
smoothly as the incentive to cooperate grows. How much of this you see depends
on how far the groups overlap, how the benefit scales with group size, and
whether players get to choose which game they play.

## Collective human behaviour
id: collective
chips: Computational social science, Behavioural experiments, Synthesis
papers: [Higher-order interactions shape collective human behaviour](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-025-02373-5) — Nat. Hum. Behav. 2025

The evidence that group interactions matter is spread thin across statistical
physics, experimental economics, social psychology and computational social
science, and those fields do not cite each other much.

With co-authors from several of them I tried to put it in one place: where
higher-order effects have been measured in people rather than assumed, which
models account for them, and what is still open.

## Cultural evolution & social structure
id: culture
chips: Evolutionary anthropology, Cumulative culture, Population connectivity
papers: [Population connectivity shapes the distribution and complexity of chimpanzee cumulative culture](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adk3381) — Science 2024

For knowledge to accumulate rather than stay flat, somebody has to innovate and
the innovation has to reach other people. The second half of that depends on how
connected the population is.

With evolutionary anthropologists I use genetic and ecological data to estimate
that connectivity, then ask whether it predicts how complex a population's
culture is. In chimpanzees it does: the better connected populations have richer
tool repertoires. In Zürich I am now doing the same for contemporary
hunter-gatherer societies.

## Nonequilibrium statistical physics
id: statphys
chips: Stochastic resetting, Active matter, Thermodynamic uncertainty relations
papers: [Active Brownian motion in two dimensions under stochastic resetting](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.102.052129) — PRE 2020;
  [Thermodynamic uncertainty relation for energy transport](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.103.022141) — PRE 2021;
  [Zero-current nonequilibrium state in symmetric exclusion process](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/ab9e5e) — J. Stat. Mech. 2020

My first field, and still the methods I reach for first. Stochastic processes
held away from equilibrium: a random walker that keeps getting reset to its
starting point, a self-propelled particle under the same protocol, and how far
fluctuations limit transport in very small systems.

## Networks in the wild
id: applied
chips: Sports analytics, Epidemic spreading, Mobility networks
papers: [Individual and team performance in cricket](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.240809) — R. Soc. Open Sci. 2024;
  [From streaks to synergies: performance and scoring in the NBA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27957) — arXiv 2026;
  [An infectious diseases hazard map for India](https://doi.org/10.18520/cs/v121/i9/1208-1215) — Curr. Sci. 2021

Projects I took on largely because the data was good. Pulling individual skill
apart from team context in cricket and in the NBA, and mapping how India's
transport network would carry an infectious disease.

They are a useful check on the rest. A mechanism that only shows up on synthetic
networks has not really been demonstrated.
